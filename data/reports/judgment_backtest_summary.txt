READ THIS FIRST
========================================================================
A directional call only makes sense if hit rate > 50% and/or mean
directional return > 0 (judge better than the sentiment-only baseline).
Expect the SAME-DAY rows to show a real effect (that is what the study
measured) and the NEXT-DAY rows to be statistically indistinguishable
from chance - the tool is an alignment reading, NOT a forecast.
